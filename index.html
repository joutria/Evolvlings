EvolvlingsHatch, raise and evolve a little creature of the Glimmerglade.

Settings

From above, your creature walks to the bowl, the pond or its bed when you care for it. The star catch game is always played in the side view.

Hides the Wells of Echoes for anyone who only wants to look after their creature. Song Notes you earn while it is on turn into Stardust by themselves (1 Note = 25 Stardust). The Notes you already have are kept.

Normal time means a creature grows up over a few hours and gets hungry while you're away. Fast and Turbo are handy for trying the whole life cycle quickly.

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Off: your creature never leaves. While you're away its needs never drop below a quarter, and if its health runs out it rests at the Refuge and comes back with its level and cards. On: the old rules, where a creature whose health reaches zero leaves for good.
